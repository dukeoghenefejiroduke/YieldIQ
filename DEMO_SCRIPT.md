# Winning Demo Flow: 60-Second YieldIQ Pitch

**Goal:** Convince investors that this is an essential, high-utility tool for the Nigerian market.

### Phase 1: The Problem (0-15 seconds)
*   **Visual:** Close up of your face, looking serious.
*   **Script:** "Meet the farmers who feed Nigeria. They face massive crop losses every year, not because they aren't skilled, but because they have no access to expert advice—and no internet to find it."

### Phase 2: The Solution (15-45 seconds)
*   **Visual:** Transition to a screen-recording/hands-on shot of the app.
*   **Script:** "This is YieldIQ. It works even without a signal. [Start the app] I just tap this button and speak to log a problem. [Show recording button]. YieldIQ processes this, saves it locally, and the second I walk into coverage, it syncs. It’s an expert in your pocket, that never goes offline."

### Phase 3: The Vision & CTA (45-60 seconds)
*   **Visual:** Back to your face, professional and energetic.
*   **Script:** "We’re building a trusted, voice-first digital record for the entire agricultural sector. Join us in digitizing the last mile of Nigerian farming. Let's grow together—YieldIQ."
