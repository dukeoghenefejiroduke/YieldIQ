# AgroVoice Demo Script (3-Minute)

**0:00 - 0:30 | The Hook: The Crisis**
"Meet the backbone of Nigeria's economy: the smallholder farmer. Despite producing most of our food, they are largely unbanked, lack digital history, and are vulnerable to climate risks. AgroVoice bridges this gap."

**0:30 - 1:30 | The Magic: Offline-First & Multi-Modal Input**
"AgroVoice works everywhere. Farmers log via smartphone, SMS, or USSD—even offline. *(Toggle Demo Mode)*. Watch as we log an offline sale. Instant structure, AI-parsed, and safely cached locally. When they hit a 3G zone, our resilient sync service ensures the data reaches the cloud with exponential backoff retries."

**1:30 - 2:00 | The Empowerment: Localization & Alerts**
*(Toggle Language to Hausa)*
"We speak their language—Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and Pidgin. And we provide proactive risk mitigation. *(Show Weather Alert Banner)*. If localized weather data indicates crop risk due to low rainfall, we trigger an immediate risk alert, allowing the farmer to proactively contact their cooperative for insurance."

**2:00 - 2:45 | The Outcome: Credit & Scale**
"Every interaction builds a 'digital identity'. We use this to generate AI-driven behavioral credit scores, finally unlocking formal credit without traditional collateral."

**2:45 - 3:00 | Close**
"AgroVoice isn't just an app; it's a world-class, inclusive digital ecosystem for the backbone of Nigeria. Thank you."
