# AgroVoice Demo Script (3-Minute)

**0:00 - 0:30 | The Hook: The Crisis**
"Meet the backbone of Nigeria's economy: the smallholder farmer. Despite producing most of our food, they are largely unbanked, rely on paper ledgers, and have limited access to credit. Why? Because they lack a 'digital footprint'. AgroVoice changes this."

**0:30 - 1:30 | The Magic: Demo Mode**
"AgroVoice is offline-first. Even without internet, farmers can log transactions." 
*(Toggle Demo Mode)*
"Watch what happens when a farmer speaks or sends a WhatsApp message. *(Trigger Simulation)*. Instantly, their transaction is structured, parsed locally by our AI, and stored safely."

**1:30 - 2:00 | The Empowerment: Languages & OPay**
*(Toggle Language to Izon)*
"We speak their language. Farmers interact in their native tongue, bridging the literacy gap instantly. Once they hit an internet zone, it syncs. And with our integrated OPay infrastructure, they get instant, secure payment confirmations."

**2:00 - 2:45 | The Outcome: Credit & Scale**
"By logging these transactions, farmers build a 'digital identity'. We use this data to generate credit scores within farming cooperatives, finally unlocking formal financial services."

**2:45 - 3:00 | Close**
"AgroVoice isn't just an app. It's a digital bridge for the backbone of Nigeria. Thank you."
